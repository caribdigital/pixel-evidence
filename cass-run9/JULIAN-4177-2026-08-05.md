# For Julian — one question, #4177: does bad-debt relief move the net?

**From:** Robbie (engineering) · **Date:** 2026-08-05
**Issue:** #4177 (HIGH) · **Nothing has been changed in code pending your answer.**

---

## The question

**When a period contains bad-debt relief or bad-debt recovery, does that amount change the VAT payable for the period — or does it sit on the L7 adjustment line without moving the net?**

That is the whole question. Everything below is the worked case and why it matters.

---

## The worked case

February, as it stands (this is the consumed credit-note branch you and Cass already have):

| line | amount |
|---|---|
| Output VAT | 550.00 |
| Input VAT | 300.00 |
| **L7 adjustment** | **−50.00** (the credit note) |
| **Net payable** | **250.00** |

The credit note is uncontroversial: 550 already has the −50 inside it, because a credit note is an output transaction carrying negative VAT. So 550 − 300 = 250, and L7 records the −50 separately for the statutory line. Both agree.

**Now add a second component to L7.** Suppose the same period also carried **−100.00 of bad-debt relief**. Bad-debt relief is *not* a transaction in the output set — it is derived separately — so it is **not** inside the 550.

L7 would then be **−150.00** (−50 credit note, −100 bad-debt relief).

**Is the net payable 250.00 or 150.00?**

- **250.00** — bad-debt relief is disclosed on L7 but does not reduce what is payable for the period.
- **150.00** — bad-debt relief reduces the VAT payable, so the net must fall by 100.

---

## Why I am asking rather than deciding

The product currently answers this **inconsistently**, and I could make both surfaces agree in either direction with equal ease. That is exactly why it should not be my call:

- The **return generator** computes net as `output − input` and never adds L7. On that formula, bad-debt relief **never** affects the net anywhere in the product.
- The **edit-return save path** computes `(output + L7) − input`. On that formula it does.

One of them has been wrong, and which one depends entirely on your answer. If the answer is **250**, the generator is right and the save path has a bug. If the answer is **150**, the save path's *shape* is right and **the generator has been understating or overstating the net for every period containing bad-debt relief or recovery** — which would be the more serious finding, and would need a look at how far back it goes.

There is a second-order point either way: the credit note must not be counted twice. It is already inside the 550, so whatever the rule is, applying L7 wholesale to the net would double-count that −50. If the answer is 150, the correct formula is probably not "add all of L7" but "add the components of L7 that are not already in output".

---

## What is not in question

The **L7 line itself** is not in dispute. It is derived, correct, and now read-only (F-257, your and Cass's ruling of 2026-08-03). This is only about whether that figure participates in the **net payable** calculation.
